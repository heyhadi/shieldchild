Clear rules for your child. Full control for you.

Hard to remove

Device Administrator blocks uninstalling, and a guard sends ShieldChild's App info, Accessibility and Device admin screens back to Home and locks the phone. Only you can remove it, with your PIN.

Bedtime lock

Lock the whole phone every night, e.g. 8:00 PM – 7:00 AM, across midnight. The lock comes back after a reboot, and changing the clock to skip it locks the phone instead.

Extra time, your way

Signing in never gives time by itself. Choose: use the phone yourself, +15 min, +30 min, +1 hour, or no limit for the rest of today. Your child gets a warning before extra time ends.

App limits

Give games, YouTube or TikTok their own daily limit, or block them completely. Other apps keep working.

Instant lock

One switch locks the phone right now, for dinner or homework. Only you can turn it off.

Calls always work

Emergency calls use Android's own emergency dialer (112, 911, 995…). Add your number for Call parent and Text parent. An incoming call is never covered by the lock.

Remote unlock code

Away from home? Read your child the 6-digit code from Google Authenticator. It unlocks for 15 minutes, works once, max 3 times a day, and needs no internet on either phone.

Unlock log

Every unlock and every bit of extra time is listed with how and when it happened, so you notice if your PIN has leaked.

100% on the phone

No account, no servers, no analytics. Rules, screen time and the unlock log never leave the phone. The only network request is the update check you start yourself.

Install on your child's phone in 2 minutes.

ShieldChild isn't on the Play Store; you install it from GitHub. After that it updates itself from Settings.

  1. 1. Install the app

    On your child's phone, download shieldchild.apk from the latest release and tap it to install. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser.

    "Unsafe app blocked" or "App scan recommended"? That's Google Play Protect, which warns about every app that isn't on the Play Store. Tap More details, then Install anyway. Leave Play Protect itself turned on.

  2. 2. Set up your child and your PIN

    Enter your child's name and daily allowance, then your PIN, optional fingerprint and your phone number for "Call parent". Your PIN is only saved when you tap Start protecting at the end, so finish all the steps.

  3. 3. Allow the permissions

    Usage access (measures screen time), Accessibility (notices app switches, guards ShieldChild's settings), Display over other apps (the lock screen), Device admin (blocks uninstalling) and Notifications.

    Accessibility switch greyed out ("Restricted setting")? Android 13 and newer does this for apps installed from a file. Open Settings → Apps → ShieldChild, tap ⋮ (top right) → Allow restricted settings, then go back and tap Grant. If ⋮ has no such option, tap the greyed-out switch once first. ShieldChild shows these steps with a button, too.

    On a child account supervised with Google Family Link, you may need to allow this from the Family Link app on your phone.

  4. 4. Write down your recovery key

    ShieldChild shows a 16-character recovery key once. It resets your PIN if you forget it. Optionally, set up the remote unlock code with Google Authenticator.

What it protects against, honestly.

No app on a normal Android phone is impossible to get around. Here is what ShieldChild stops, and what needs the stronger Device Owner mode.

Stopped on every phone

Uninstalling, force-stopping or clearing ShieldChild; swiping Home or opening Recents while locked; rebooting to skip the lock; changing the clock or time zone (with automatic time off, this locks the phone); guessing the PIN (5 tries, then 15 minutes); a fingerprint added later; screenshots of the PIN and recovery key screens.

Needs Device Owner mode

Safe mode, switching to another user profile, and app-data tricks through adb. Device Owner is set up once with a computer on a phone with no accounts added yet; the steps are in the README.

Out of scope

A factory reset removes every app, including ShieldChild. A second phone or a friend's phone isn't covered.

Your PIN and keys

Your PIN and recovery key are stored only as PBKDF2 hashes. The remote-code secret is encrypted with a key kept in Android's Keystore. Updates are installed only if they're signed by the same developer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my child uninstall ShieldChild while they still have screen time?

No. While Device Administrator is active Android won't uninstall it, and the screens to turn that off are sent back to Home, also when your child still has screen time left.

How can a parent uninstall ShieldChild when needed?

Parents can uninstall cleanly at any time directly through the app. Open ShieldChild, authenticate with your Master PIN or fingerprint, go to the Security tab, and select Uninstall ShieldChild. After you confirm with your PIN, Android's uninstall screen opens.

What if my child force-restarts the phone to bypass the lock?

It doesn't help. ShieldChild checks the rules as soon as the phone starts and shows the lock screen straight away if bedtime or the daily limit applies.

What if I forget my Parent Master PIN?

ShieldChild provides a 16-character Master Recovery Key, unique to your phone and shown once during setup. On the lock screen, tap Forgot PIN? Use Recovery Key, enter your recovery key, and choose a new PIN. The device resets and unlocks immediately, completely offline.

Does ShieldChild track my family's data or require an account?

No. There's no account and no server. Everything stays on the phone. ShieldChild only goes online when you tap "Check for updates" in Settings.

Which Android devices and versions are supported?

Android 8 or newer. It's tested on a OnePlus 6 running LineageOS (Android 15). Other phones should work; some manufacturers stop background apps aggressively, so allow ShieldChild to run without battery restrictions.

Can I give extra time when I'm not at home?

Yes. Turn on the remote unlock code in Security and scan the QR code with Google Authenticator on your phone. When your child calls, read them the current 6-digit code. It works once, gives 15 minutes by default, and needs no internet on either phone.

Does it work with several user profiles on one phone?

ShieldChild protects the profile it's installed in. If the phone has other user profiles, your child could switch to one; Device Owner mode blocks adding and switching users. Install and set it up in the profile your child uses.

Is it available in Indonesian?

Yes. Choose Bahasa Indonesia in Settings → Appearance → Language, independent of the phone's language.
